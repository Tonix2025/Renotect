FAQ

Common questions

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first consultation is free and comes with no obligation. We review your space or plans, talk through your goals, budget and timing, and explain what the next steps would be.

How much does a renovation cost?

It depends on the size, the scope and the finishes you choose. After the consultation we give you a clear picture of what your project involves so you can plan a realistic budget.

Do I need a permit for my renovation?

Many projects do, especially those that move walls, change plumbing or electrical, or alter the structure. We tell you early which approvals your project needs and prepare the drawings for them.

Do you only work on full homes?

No. We take on single rooms such as kitchens and bathrooms as well as full-home projects.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Etobicoke and work across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area, including North York, Mississauga and Vaughan.
