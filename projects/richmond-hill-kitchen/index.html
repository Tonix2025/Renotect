Richmond Hill Kitchen

A modern kitchen for a new custom home in Richmond Hill. Two finishes do most of the work: matte grey flat-panel cabinetry for the tall walls and perimeter, and warm walnut-tone wood for the island and the upper cabinets either side of the window wall. A large island wrapped in light veined stone, with waterfall ends to the floor, sits under a stepped ceiling. The same stone runs up the backsplash and clads the range hood, which is framed by two tall black windows. Tall cabinets keep the fridge and storage built in, and a walnut display unit with glass shelves closes the kitchen off from the next room. Renotect designed the kitchen and followed the work on site through installation.
