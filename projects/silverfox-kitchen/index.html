Silverfox Place kitchen, Vaughan

The kitchen of the Silverfox Place custom home in Vaughan. The concept pairs muted sage green shaker cabinetry with warm wood, light quartz and marble, and aged brass, for a calm room that will still feel right in twenty years. A long island with seating faces the cooking wall, tall cabinets keep the fridge, ovens and pantry built in flush, and a long picture window opens the room to the garden. The photos follow it from the first renders to cabinets and counters going in.
