Custom home facade, North York

Exterior design for a new custom home on McGillivray Avenue in North York. The front elevation pairs smooth limestone-style cladding with a dark slate mansard roof, tall black-framed windows and a double garage. Lighting was planned as part of the design, with soffit pot lights and wall sconces that frame the windows after dark.
