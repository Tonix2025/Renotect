Sage green kitchen with island

A kitchen planned around a long island with seating for four. Sage green shaker cabinets run to the ceiling, with the fridge, wall oven and coffee station built in flush. A tray ceiling with cove lighting, under-cabinet LEDs and glass pendants layer the light for cooking and for evenings.
