Silverfox Place custom home, Vaughan

A new modern home of about 6,000 sq ft on Silverfox Place in Vaughan, Ontario, with a low flat roof, deep overhangs, large black-framed windows and a walk-out lower level. Renotect designed the interiors and the exterior facade, and oversaw the work on site through construction so the build followed the design.
