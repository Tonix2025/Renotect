Silverfox Place custom home, Vaughan

A modern home of about 6,000 sq ft on Silverfox Place in Vaughan, Ontario. The facade is built from clean geometry and a tight palette: black aluminum cladding frames the structure, floated wood panels add warmth and a vertical rhythm to the upper floor, a white stone base grounds the house, and silver aluminum trim sharpens the edges. Wall-mounted lights wash the materials and mark the entries after dark. Renotect designed the interiors and the facade, and oversaw the work on site through construction so the build followed the design.
