Permits

Does your renovation need a permit?

In Toronto and the rest of Ontario, work that changes the structure of a house, adds space, moves or adds plumbing, or changes how a space is used usually needs a building permit from the municipality. Cosmetic work such as painting, new flooring or replacing cabinets in the same layout usually does not.

We tell you early which approvals your project needs, and prepare the drawings for them as part of the design. Read more about our permit drawings for houses and small buildings.

FAQ

Renovation questions

How long does a full-home renovation take?

It depends on the size of the house, the scope and the permit review. After the consultation we give you a realistic schedule for design, permits and construction.

How much does a full-home renovation cost?

The cost depends on the size, the scope and the finishes you choose. Detailed drawings and selections let contractors price the same job, so you can compare quotes and plan a realistic budget.

Do you work outside Toronto?

Yes. We are based in Etobicoke and work across the GTA, including Vaughan, North York, Mississauga, Richmond Hill, Markham and Oakville.
