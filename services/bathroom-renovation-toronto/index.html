Bathroom design

Designed for the way you use the room

A good bathroom starts with the layout. Where the shower and toilet sit, how wide the vanity can be, whether a tub is worth the space, and where the drains and vent can realistically go. Once the layout works, the finishes follow: tile, stone, fixtures and a vanity built to fit the room exactly.

In many Toronto homes the bathrooms are small, the plumbing is old and the ventilation was never enough. A renovation is the right time to fix what is behind the walls as well as what you see, from new drains and waterproofing behind the shower to a fan that actually clears the steam. For larger changes, such as moving drains, adding a bathroom or touching the structure, we prepare the permit drawings in-house.

Process

How a bathroom project works

  1. Consultation

    We look at the room, talk through what is not working, your budget and timing, and whether the work needs a permit. The first consultation is free.

  2. Layout and concept

    We measure, propose layout options and bring tile, fixtures and finishes together on a concept board.

  3. Renders and drawings

    3D renders show the finished room. Plans, elevations and tile layouts give the trades the same clear job to price.

  4. Build

    The work goes ahead with our oversight on site, from demolition and plumbing to the last fixture.

FAQ

Bathroom questions

Does a bathroom renovation need a permit in Toronto?

Replacing fixtures and finishes in the same places usually does not. Adding a bathroom, moving drains or changing the structure usually does. We check this at the start and prepare the permit drawings if they are needed.

Can you move the shower or toilet to a new spot?

Often, yes. It depends on where the drains and the vent can go and what is below the floor. We check this at the consultation before the layout is drawn.

Can you design a custom vanity?

Yes. Our vanities are designed to fit the room, with cabinetry, finishes, mirrors and lighting planned together. See some of them in our custom millwork project.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost?

It depends on the size of the room, the finishes and how much changes behind the walls. We explain the scope and the cost after the free consultation, before any work starts.

Do you renovate bathrooms as part of a larger project?

Yes. Many of our bathrooms are part of a full-home renovation or a new custom home.

Which areas do you serve?

Toronto, including North York and Etobicoke, Vaughan, Richmond Hill and the rest of the Greater Toronto Area.
